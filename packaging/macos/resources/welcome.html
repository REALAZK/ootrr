OOTRR

A randomizer for the Ocarina of Time remake.

This installer puts the ootrr command in /usr/local/bin and adds an OOTRR app to your Applications folder.

OOTRR contains no game files, no keys and no dumps. It only reads files you extracted yourself from a copy of the game you own, and it writes a mod folder that your emulator can load.

This is an early version. Back up your saves before you try a seed.
