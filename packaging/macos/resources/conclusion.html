OOTRR is installed

Open OOTRR from your Applications folder. A Terminal window opens and shows a link. Open the link in your browser to generate a seed and build a mod. Keep the Terminal window open while you use it.

You can also run ootrr serve in a terminal yourself.

This installer is not signed. If macOS says it cannot verify the app the first time, open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and choose Open Anyway.
